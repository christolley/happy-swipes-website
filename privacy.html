Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/11/2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Happy Swipes ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Username, email address, and password
  • Content: Posts you create, including text and happiness scores
  • Social Data: Friend connections and interactions

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our service
  • Allow you to create and share posts
  • Enable social features (friends, sharing)
  • Improve and optimize our app
  • Communicate with you about updates or issues
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data:

  • With Other Users: Your posts are visible according to your privacy settings (public, friends, or private)
  • Service Providers: AWS for hosting and data storage
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure. We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Secure authentication (AWS Cognito)
  • Access controls and monitoring

6. Your Privacy Choices

6.1 Post Privacy

You control who sees your posts through privacy settings:

  • Public: Visible to all users
  • Friends: Visible only to accepted friends
  • Private: Visible only to you

    • 6.2 Account and Data Deletion

    You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. You have two options:

    • In-App Deletion: Go to Settings → Delete Account within the Happy Swipes app
    • Web Request: If you cannot access the app, visit our data deletion page or email us at happyswipesapp@gmail.com

    Account deletion will permanently remove:

    • Your account and profile information
    • All your posts
    • Your friend connections

    Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. For web requests, we will verify your identity by sending a confirmation email to the address associated with your account.

    7. Children's Privacy

    Happy Swipes is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

    8. Child Safety and Compliance

    Happy Swipes is committed to compliance with child safety standards:

    • Age Requirement: Users must be at least 13 years of age to create an account and use our service
    • Age Verification: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
    • Parental Notification: If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information and terminate the account immediately
    • Content Safety: We strictly prohibit content that exploits, harms, or endangers children in any way
    • Reporting: Users can report inappropriate content or behavior within the app. Violations of child safety policies will result in immediate account termination and may be reported to authorities

    Parents or guardians who believe their child under 13 has created an account should contact us immediately at happyswipesapp@gmail.com for account removal.

    9. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
